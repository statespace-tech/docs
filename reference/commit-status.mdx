---
title: Commit status
description: Each state and description of the statespace/<app>/<experiment> commit status on an arm's commit.
---

Statespace posts a commit status on the commit of each arm. There is one status for each app and experiment. Its context is `statespace/<app>/<experiment>`, such as `statespace/shop-prod/checkout`.

- Statespace posts at each change of state: start, stop, removal, and an arm that is not ready.
- While the experiment runs, Statespace posts once an hour, only when the description changed.
- Statespace posts only for arms of running experiments, and one last time at stop or removal. Old lines stay as they were last written.
- GitHub accepts 1,000 statuses for each commit and context at most. Statespace stops the hourly posts at 950, to keep room for changes of state.
- The description has 140 characters at most.
- **Details** opens the experiment in the dashboard: `https://statespace.com/dashboard/apps/<app>/experiments/<experiment>?workspace=<login>`.
- The state says where the arm is. It never says that the arm is good or bad. It is never `failure`.

## States

| The arm | State | Description, for example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Runs | `pending` | `v4 running · 0.05 · signup 0.0261 [0.0184, 0.0368] · 1,188 subjects · split OK` |
| Runs, the split is off | `pending` | `v4 running · 0.05 · signup 0.0261 [0.0184, 0.0368] · 1,188 subjects · split off` |
| Runs, not ready | `pending` | `v4 not ready: the container exits (exit 1) · subjects go to live` |
| Is stopped | `success` | `v4 stopped · signup 0.0261 [0.0184, 0.0368] · 1,188 subjects` |
| Is stopped by a release | `success` | `v4 stopped: definition changed in live d00d123 · signup 0.0261 [0.0184, 0.0368] · 1,188 subjects` |
| Is removed | `success` | `removed · last v4 · signup 0.0261 [0.0184, 0.0368] · 1,188 subjects` |

## The running description

The description of an arm in a running experiment has these parts, in this order:

1. the version, such as `v4`;
2. `running`;
3. the share of the arm;
4. the headline, such as `signup 0.0261 [0.0184, 0.0368]`;
5. the subjects of the arm, such as `1,188 subjects`;
6. the split check, `split OK` or `split off`, from 100 subjects in all arms.

Every arm's line says `running`. No arm is special.

## The headline

- The headline is the first event of the version's `statespace.toml`. It is the share of the subjects of the arm with one or more matches, as a fraction with 4 decimals, and its 95% interval.
- When the version has no events, the headline is `requests_5xx`: the share of the subjects with one or more responses with a status from 500 to 599, such as `requests_5xx 0.0438 [0.0331, 0.0578] · 1,188 subjects`.
- Each line has the numbers of its own arm only.
- Below 30 subjects, the interval is `[–]`, such as `lead 0.0000 [–] · 3 subjects`.
- Without a value, the headline shows `–`, such as `lead – · 0 subjects`.
- One subject shows as `1 subject`.
- The line of a stopped or removed arm shows the snapshot of its version.

## Permission

The Statespace GitHub App needs only the permission to write commit statuses for this. Your CI does nothing for it.

---
title: Commit status
description: The states and descriptions of the statespace/<app>/<experiment> commit status on the commit of each arm.
---

Statespace posts a commit status on the commit of each arm. There is one status for each app and experiment. Its context is `statespace/<app>/<experiment>`, such as `statespace/shop-prod/checkout`. Your CI does nothing for it.

## States

| The arm | State | Description, for example |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Runs | `pending` | `v4 running · 0.05 · signup 0.0261 [0.0184, 0.0368] · 1,188 subjects · split OK` |
| Runs, the split is off | `pending` | `v4 running · 0.05 · signup 0.0261 [0.0184, 0.0368] · 1,188 subjects · split off` |
| Runs, not ready | `pending` | `v4 not ready: the container exits (exit 1) · subjects go to live` |
| Is stopped | `success` | `v4 stopped · signup 0.0261 [0.0184, 0.0368] · 1,188 subjects` |
| Is stopped by a release | `success` | `v4 stopped: definition changed in live d00d123 · signup 0.0261 [0.0184, 0.0368] · 1,188 subjects` |
| Is removed | `success` | `removed · last v4 · signup 0.0261 [0.0184, 0.0368] · 1,188 subjects` |

The state tells where the arm is. It never tells that the arm is good or bad, and it is never `failure`.

## Description

The description of a running arm has these parts, in this order:

1. The version, such as `v4`, and `running`. Every arm says `running`.
2. The share of the arm, such as `0.05`.
3. The headline, such as `signup 0.0261 [0.0184, 0.0368]`.
4. The subjects of the arm, such as `1,188 subjects`.
5. The split check, `split OK` or `split off`. It shows from 100 subjects in all arms.

The headline is the first event of the version's `statespace.toml`: the share of the subjects of the arm with one or more matches, with 4 decimals, and its 95% interval. Without events, the headline is `requests_5xx`. See [Events and results](/concepts/events-and-results).

- Each line has the numbers of its own arm only.
- Below 30 subjects, the interval is `[–]`, such as `lead 0.0000 [–] · 3 subjects`.
- Without a value, the headline is `–`, such as `lead – · 0 subjects`.
- The line of a stopped or removed arm shows the final results of its version.
- The description has 140 characters at most.

## When Statespace posts

- At each change of state: start, stop, removal, and an arm that is not ready.
- Once an hour while the experiment runs, only when the description changed.
- Only for the arms of running experiments, and one last time at stop or removal. Older statuses stay as they were.

GitHub accepts at most 1,000 statuses for each commit and context. Statespace stops the hourly posts at 950, to keep room for changes of state.

**Details** opens the experiment in the dashboard: `https://statespace.com/dashboard/apps/<app>/experiments/<experiment>?workspace=<login>`. The Statespace GitHub App needs only the permission to write commit statuses.

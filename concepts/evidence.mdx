title: Evidence on the commit

Where do you and your agents read the evidence of an arm?

Statespace posts a commit status on each arm's commit, for each app. Its name is `statespace/<app>`, such as `statespace/shop-prod`. Statespace updates the status at each change, and about every 10 minutes.

```text
statespace/shop-prod  pending
5% · 1,204 subjects · split OK · checkout rate % 3.4 vs 2.8
· errors 0.3% vs 0.2%
```

The running line has:

1. the share of the arm;
2. the subjects of the arm;
3. the [split check](/concepts/events-and-metrics#the-split-check);
4. the first metric, arm vs control;
5. the errors, arm vs control.

## The state says where the arm is

The state of the status says where the arm is. It never says that the arm is good or bad.

| The arm | State |
| --- | --- |
| Starts, waits, or runs | `pending` |
| Is not ready, or is stopped | `failure` |
| Is removed | `success` |

See [Commit status](/reference/commit-status) for each description.

## More evidence

- **Details** on the status opens the arm in the dashboard.
- `ssp arm show <commit> --app <app>` shows the totals and all metrics for each side, the split check, the top events, the versions, and the holds.
- `ssp arm show --json` gives the same data to agents.

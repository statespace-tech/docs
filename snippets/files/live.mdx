Statespace finds the live commit from the files at the root of the bucket:

1. The proxy lists the root of the bucket.
2. It leaves out each folder that holds recorded builds of the app, such as `builds/`.
3. It computes the files hash of the other files, and reports it.
4. Statespace finds the build with that hash. Its commit is the live commit.

So the files at the root must be the files of a recorded build. Put all builds in one folder, such as `builds/`. Do not add or change files at the root after the deploy: then the hash matches no build. While the hash matches no build, the app has no live commit, and no experiment can start. `ssp app show` says why.

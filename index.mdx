Statespace runs commits of your app on small shares of real traffic. It records what happens, and puts the evidence on each commit as a commit status.

An **experiment** compares commits. Each commit is an **arm** with an exact share of the visitors, such as `0.05`. To compare a change with what runs now, make the live commit one of the arms. The proxy in front of your app sends each arm its share. All other visitors get your live service, as before.

<TrafficDiagram />

Your CI builds the images. Statespace runs only images of builds that your CI recorded. Your settings, events, metrics, and experiments are in `statespace.toml`, in your repo.

## How you use it

1. Your CI builds each push and records the build with `statespace-tech/action@v1`.
2. `ssp proxy` runs in front of your app.
3. The table `[experiment.checkout]` in `statespace.toml` defines an experiment.
4. You set the arms with `ssp experiment update checkout --arm c0ffee1=0.05 --arm a1b2c3d=0.05`.
5. You start the experiment with `ssp experiment start checkout`.
6. The commit status `statespace/shop-prod/checkout` on each arm's commit shows the evidence: subjects, the split check, and your headline metric.
7. You stop the experiment with `ssp experiment stop checkout`.

Statespace shows numbers for each arm. It does not tell you which arm is better. You and your agents decide.
